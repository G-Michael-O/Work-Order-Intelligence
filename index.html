Investigation prompts

HVAC: Do repeated reports relate to the same equipment, location or operating condition?

Plumbing: Are repeated reports associated with the same fixture, drain or unit?

Appliances: Are recurring tickets linked to the same appliance or equipment age?

Pest Control: Do reports cluster by location, season or building condition?

AI-assisted analysis: proposed future capability

An approved AI service could assist with categorising free-text descriptions, grouping similar reports, identifying possible repeated issues, summarising patterns, highlighting incomplete information and preparing an investigation report.

This application does not connect to AI. AI must not be represented as having diagnosed a technical cause.

Human validation requirements

  • Qualified maintenance professionals validate technical diagnoses and interventions.
  • Property operators verify work-order history, asset information and locations.
  • Finance validates invoices, costs and accounting treatment.
  • Management approves preventative schedules and budget changes.

Proposed workflow architecture

Maintenance Request→Work Order Created→Data Quality Review→Categorisation→Pattern Analysis→Human Investigation→Professional Validation→Action Approval→Budget Forecast→Ongoing Monitoring

The PMS should remain the primary operational record where appropriate. Potential future layers may include PMS-native automation, Zapier or Make for approved cross-application workflows, Airtable for structured information where justified, Process Street for documented checklists, Slack for internal notifications, and Mailchimp only for an approved communication use case. Do not force these products into the solution.

Before integrations are promised, verify API availability, permissions, plan eligibility, triggers, actions, licensing, data security and applicable rate limits. First establish whether the PMS already supports the required functionality.

Property Management Process Discovery, Systems Assessment and Workflow Improvement

A practical service process: understand existing processes; assess software and information flows; identify verified gaps and opportunities; design improved workflows; document repeatable procedures; implement appropriate automation where justified; monitor performance and refine the process.

This is operational improvement and workflow automation support, not a replacement for property managers, accountants, engineers or maintenance professionals.

Privacy warning: Do not enter resident names, personal contact information, access codes, bank information, credentials or confidential client records into a public demonstration or unapproved AI service. Imported data remains within this browser session.
